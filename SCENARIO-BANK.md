## How to Use This Bank

1. Pick a domain matching your current study week
2. Work through scenarios in order — Medium first, then Hard, then Expert
3. For **Design** scenarios: draw it out (Excalidraw) + narrate out loud
4. For **Troubleshoot** scenarios: write the commands you'd run before looking up answers
5. For **Code** scenarios: solve in a timed environment (30–45 min)
6. Mark ✅ only when you can answer without hesitation, not just when you've seen it
7. Revisit 🟡 items weekly until they become ✅
