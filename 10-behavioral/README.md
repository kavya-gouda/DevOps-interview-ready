## The STAR Format (Senior Level)

At 12 years of experience, your stories need **scale and ambiguity**. Junior STAR = "I fixed a bug". Senior STAR = "I led the re-architecture."

```
Situation  → Context (team size, scale, company stage)
Task       → Your specific responsibility / what was at stake
Action     → What YOU did (not "we") — decisions, trade-offs, leadership
Result     → Quantified outcome + what you learned
```

**Senior story minimum bar:**
- Affects 5+ engineers, or engineering-wide process, or business-critical system
- Shows decision-making under uncertainty or imperfect information
- Has a measurable outcome (% uptime improvement, cost reduction, MTTR reduction, time saved)
- Reveals your thinking about trade-offs, not just execution

## High-Frequency Questions (Senior DevOps)

### Technical
- "Tell me about the most complex infrastructure you've designed."
- "How did you improve reliability in your last role?"
- "Walk me through a production incident you led."
- "How do you evaluate a new technology before adopting it?"
- "What's your approach to migrating a legacy system to cloud-native?"

### Leadership & Influence
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision and what you did."
- "How do you get buy-in from engineers who don't want to change?"
- "Describe a time you had to balance velocity vs reliability."
- "Tell me about a time you mentored someone and changed their trajectory."

### Ambiguity & Decision-Making
- "Tell me about a time you had to make a significant decision with incomplete data."
- "Describe a project where scope or requirements kept changing."
- "When have you had to prioritise ruthlessly? What did you cut?"

### Failure
- "Tell me about your biggest professional failure."
- "Describe a production incident you caused or contributed to."
- "What would you do differently in your last role?"

## Delivery Principles

- **Under 3 minutes per story** — practice out loud, time yourself
- **"I", not "we"** — they're evaluating you
- **Name the trade-off** — "we could have done X, but we chose Y because..."
- **Show self-awareness** — what you'd do differently
- **Quantify** — even rough numbers beat "significantly improved"
